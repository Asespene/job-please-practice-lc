# Behavioral Interview Prep & STAR Story Bank

Prepare **6–8 versatile stories** that can be adapted to any behavioral prompt. Aim for **90–120 seconds** per response:
- **S — Situation (10%):** 1–2 sentences setting context (team, product, scale/stakes).
- **T — Task (10%):** The specific problem, constraint, or ambiguity you were responsible for.
- **A — Action (60%):** 3 concrete steps **you** took ("I analyzed...", "I proposed...", "I implemented..."). Focus on technical judgment, communication, and trade-offs.
- **R — Result (20%):** Quantified impact (latency/cost/reliability metrics, delivery speed) + what you learned.
